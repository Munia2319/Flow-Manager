WAREHOUSE OPERATIONS
Standard Operating Procedures
Document ID: SOP-WH-001
Version 3.1 - January 2025

========================================
PURPOSE
========================================

This document establishes standard procedures for all warehouse operations including receiving, storage, picking, packing, and shipping. Following these procedures ensures:

- Accurate inventory management
- Safe working environment
- Efficient order fulfillment
- Customer satisfaction
- Regulatory compliance

========================================
SCOPE
========================================

This procedure applies to:
- All warehouse personnel
- Material handling equipment operators
- Inventory control staff
- Shipping and receiving clerks
- Quality control inspectors
- Supervisors and managers

========================================
GENERAL WAREHOUSE RULES
========================================

SAFETY REQUIREMENTS:

REQUIRED PPE:
- Steel-toed safety boots (always)
- High-visibility safety vest (always)
- Safety glasses in designated areas
- Hard hat in loading dock areas
- Hearing protection (if noise over 85 dB)
- Cut-resistant gloves (when handling sharp materials)

PROHIBITED ITEMS:
- Open-toed shoes or sandals
- Loose clothing that could catch
- Jewelry (rings, necklaces, bracelets)
- Headphones or earbuds
- Personal electronic devices during operation
- Food or beverages in work areas

HOUSEKEEPING STANDARDS:
- Keep aisles clear at all times
- Minimum 4-foot aisle width
- No obstructed emergency exits
- Spills cleaned immediately
- Trash removed daily
- Tools returned after use
- Pallets stacked properly

TRAFFIC FLOW:
- Designated pedestrian walkways
- Forklift lanes marked
- Speed limit: 5 MPH
- Yield to pedestrians always
- Sound horn at intersections
- No running in warehouse
- Stay within marked areas

========================================
RECEIVING PROCEDURES
========================================

RECEIVING HOURS:
Monday - Friday: 7:00 AM - 3:00 PM
Closed for lunch: 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM
No weekend receiving without pre-approval

APPOINTMENT SYSTEM:
All deliveries require appointment:
1. Carrier calls 24 hours in advance
2. Receiving schedules appointment
3. Confirmation sent to carrier
4. Dock door assigned
5. 30-minute unloading window

WALK-INS:
Non-appointment deliveries:
- Accepted only if capacity available
- May wait up to 2 hours
- Priority to scheduled deliveries

RECEIVING PROCESS:

STEP 1: CARRIER CHECK-IN
1. Driver reports to receiving office
2. Present delivery documents:
   - Bill of Lading (BOL)
   - Packing slip
   - Purchase order number
3. Driver signs in visitor log
4. Assigned to dock door
5. Safety briefing given

STEP 2: TRAILER INSPECTION
Before unloading, inspect:
□ Trailer condition (no damage)
□ Seal intact (record seal number)
□ No signs of tampering
□ Temperature (if refrigerated)
□ Cleanliness (no contamination)
□ No pest infestation

IF ISSUES FOUND:
- Photograph damage
- Note on BOL
- Notify supervisor
- May refuse delivery
- Document reason

STEP 3: UNLOADING
1. Park forklift safely
2. Chock trailer wheels
3. Lower dock plate
4. Turn on dock lights
5. Unload systematically (front to back)
6. Place goods in receiving staging area
7. Keep products organized by PO
8. Do not block aisles

SAFETY DURING UNLOADING:
- Never enter trailer without communication
- Use spotter if visibility limited
- Watch for shifting loads
- Maintain 3-point contact on trailer
- Never walk under raised forks
- Wear hard hat in trailer

STEP 4: COUNT AND INSPECT
1. Count all cartons/pallets
2. Verify quantity matches BOL
3. Inspect for visible damage:
   - Crushed boxes
   - Water damage
   - Torn packaging
   - Leaking containers
4. Photograph any damage
5. Note discrepancies on BOL

DISCREPANCY HANDLING:
OVERAGE: Accept and note on BOL
SHORTAGE: Note exact shortage, driver must sign
DAMAGE: Photograph, segregate, note on BOL, claim process

STEP 5: DOCUMENTATION
1. Sign and date BOL
2. Note any exceptions
3. Provide driver with signed copy
4. Scan documents to system
5. Enter receipt in WMS (Warehouse Management System)
6. Create quality inspection ticket if needed

RECEIVING QUALITY INSPECTION:

INSPECT RANDOM SAMPLES:
- 10% of cartons for orders under 50 units
- 5% of cartons for orders over 50 units
- 100% for high-value items
- All hazardous materials

INSPECTION CRITERIA:
□ Correct product (match PO)
□ Correct quantity
□ Acceptable condition
□ Proper labeling
□ Expiration dates acceptable
□ Packaging intact
□ No substitutions without approval

IF QUALITY FAIL:
1. Quarantine product (red tag)
2. Photograph issues
3. Notify purchasing
4. Complete non-conformance report
5. Await disposition (return, accept, or scrap)

STEP 6: PUT AWAY
1. Generate put-away task in WMS
2. Verify location assignment
3. Transport to location
4. Scan location barcode
5. Confirm put-away in system
6. Update inventory
7. Maximum put-away time: 2 hours from receipt

========================================
STORAGE PROCEDURES
========================================

LOCATION SYSTEM:

FORMAT: AA-BB-CC-DD
- AA: Aisle (01-30)
- BB: Bay (01-50)
- CC: Level (01-06)
- DD: Position (01-04)

Example: 12-25-03-02
Aisle 12, Bay 25, Level 3, Position 2

STORAGE ZONES:

ZONE A - Fast Movers:
- Near shipping area
- High-turnover items
- Easy access locations
- Ground level preferred

ZONE B - Medium Movers:
- Mid-warehouse
- Moderate turnover
- Standard racking
- Levels 1-4

ZONE C - Slow Movers:
- Back of warehouse
- Low turnover
- Upper levels acceptable
- Long-term storage

ZONE D - Bulk Storage:
- Floor-stacked pallets
- Same-SKU pallets
- Deep lane storage
- FIFO management

ZONE E - Hazmat:
- Segregated area
- Caged and locked
- Fire suppression
- Spill containment
- Limited access

RACKING RULES:

LOAD LIMITS:
- Check load capacity placard
- Never exceed rated capacity
- Heaviest items on lower levels
- Lighter items on upper levels

STACKING HEIGHTS:
- Maximum 6 pallets high (standard racking)
- Maximum 20 feet floor stacking
- Leave 18 inches clearance from sprinklers
- Maintain structural integrity

PALLET STANDARDS:
- Use 40" × 48" GMA pallets
- Four-way entry preferred
- Good condition only
- No broken boards
- Maximum weight: 2,500 lbs per pallet

PRODUCT ORIENTATION:
- Labels facing outward
- Arrows pointing up
- Fragile labels visible
- FIFO dates visible
- Lot codes accessible

INVENTORY ACCURACY:

CYCLE COUNTING:
- A Items: Weekly (high value, 20% of SKUs)
- B Items: Monthly (medium value, 30% of SKUs)
- C Items: Quarterly (low value, 50% of SKUs)

CYCLE COUNT PROCEDURE:
1. Generate count sheet from WMS
2. Print blank count form
3. Physically count product
4. Record actual quantity
5. Enter into WMS
6. Investigate discrepancies over 2%
7. Adjust inventory with supervisor approval

INVENTORY ADJUSTMENTS:
Require:
- Reason code
- Supervisor authorization
- Supporting documentation
- Root cause analysis if over $500

LOCATION AUDITS:
- Random location checks daily
- Full location audit annually
- Document findings
- Correct errors immediately
- Track accuracy metrics

Target Accuracy: 99.5% or better

========================================
PICKING PROCEDURES
========================================

ORDER PICKING METHODS:

DISCRETE PICKING (Default):
- One order at a time
- Pick complete order
- Used for large orders
- Highest accuracy

BATCH PICKING:
- Multiple orders simultaneously
- Same SKU, different orders
- Increases efficiency
- Requires sorting after pick

WAVE PICKING:
- Scheduled pick waves
- All orders in time window
- Consolidated picking
- Used during peak times

ZONE PICKING:
- Each picker assigned zone
- Orders passed between zones
- Reduces travel time
- Requires coordination

PICKING PROCESS:

STEP 1: RECEIVE PICK TICKET
1. Log into RF scanner
2. Receive pick assignment
3. Review order requirements:
   - Priority level
   - Special instructions
   - Shipping method
   - Customer requirements
4. Gather materials (cart, boxes, tape)

STEP 2: TRAVEL TO LOCATION
1. Follow efficient route
2. Scan location barcode
3. Verify location on screen
4. Visual confirmation of product

STEP 3: PICK PRODUCT
1. Verify correct item:
   - Check SKU number
   - Match product description
   - Verify lot/serial if required
2. Count accurately
3. Inspect for damage
4. Scan product barcode
5. Confirm quantity picked
6. Remove from location

STEP 4: QUALITY CHECK
For each pick:
□ Correct item number
□ Correct quantity
□ Good condition
□ No damage or defects
□ Within expiration date
□ Proper packaging

STEP 5: COMPLETE PICK
1. Place in tote/cart
2. Keep order separate
3. Include pick ticket
4. Complete all line items
5. Scan "complete" in system
6. Transport to packing area

PICKING RULES:

FIFO (First In, First Out):
- Pick oldest stock first
- Check production/expiration dates
- Rotate stock properly
- Exception: LIFO if specified

ACCURACY STANDARDS:
- 99.8% picking accuracy required
- Double-check on high-value items
- Supervisory verify over $1,000
- Zero tolerance for wrong product

SHORT PICKS:
If item not in location:
1. Check adjacent locations
2. Verify WMS shows inventory
3. Search immediate area
4. If not found, create shortage ticket
5. Notify supervisor
6. System generates recount task

PARTIAL PICKS:
If insufficient quantity:
1. Pick available quantity
2. Note shortage in system
3. Create backorder
4. Notify customer service
5. Suggest substitutions if available

========================================
PACKING PROCEDURES
========================================

PACKING STATIONS:

REQUIRED SUPPLIES:
- Corrugated boxes (various sizes)
- Bubble wrap
- Packing peanuts
- Air pillows
- Tape (3-inch reinforced)
- Tape gun
- Box cutters
- Shipping labels
- Packing slips
- Scales
- Dimensional scanner

PACKING PROCESS:

STEP 1: VERIFY ORDER
1. Receive picked order at station
2. Scan order barcode
3. Review packing instructions
4. Check for special requirements:
   - Gift wrap
   - Special labeling
   - Hazmat requirements
   - Insurance

STEP 2: SELECT PACKAGING
1. Choose appropriate box size
2. Minimize void space
3. Consider product fragility
4. Check weight limits
5. Use manufacturer packaging when possible

BOX SIZING GUIDELINES:
- Small: Under 5 lbs, under 12" any dimension
- Medium: 5-15 lbs, under 18" any dimension
- Large: 15-40 lbs, under 24" any dimension
- Extra Large: Over 40 lbs, oversized

STEP 3: PACK ITEMS
1. Line box with cushioning if needed
2. Place heavier items on bottom
3. Wrap fragile items individually
4. Fill void space with packing material
5. Items should not shift when shaken
6. Do not overfill box

PACKING STANDARDS:
- 2-inch cushioning on all sides (fragile)
- No items touching box sides (fragile)
- Proper orientation (arrows up)
- Crush-proof arrangement
- Secure all moving parts

STEP 4: VERIFY CONTENTS
1. Compare to pick ticket
2. Count each item
3. Check for damage
4. Verify all items present
5. Quality inspection

FINAL CHECK:
□ All items in box
□ No damage
□ Properly cushioned
□ Nothing missing
□ Special requirements met

STEP 5: SEAL AND LABEL
1. Close box flaps
2. Apply tape:
   - H-taping method preferred
   - Tape all seams
   - Reinforce bottom
3. Weigh package
4. Measure dimensions
5. Print shipping label
6. Apply label to top of box
7. Apply packing slip
8. Apply special labels if needed:
   - Fragile
   - This Side Up
   - Do Not Stack
   - Hazmat placards

STEP 6: SCAN AND STAGE
1. Scan shipping label
2. Confirm ship method
3. Place in staging area by carrier:
   - UPS zone
   - FedEx zone
   - LTL freight zone
   - Will call area
4. Update system status to "packed"

PACKAGING GUIDELINES:

FRAGILE ITEMS:
- Double-box if highly fragile
- 3-inch cushioning minimum
- Mark "FRAGILE" on all sides
- Include handling instructions
- Photograph high-value items

LIQUIDS:
- Verify cap is tight
- Seal in plastic bag
- Absorbent material inside
- Waterproof inner container
- Mark "LIQUID" on box

HAZARDOUS MATERIALS:
- Follow DOT regulations
- Proper packaging required
- Hazmat certification needed
- Special labeling mandatory
- MSDS sheet included
- Limited quantity markings

INTERNATIONAL SHIPMENTS:
- Commercial invoice (3 copies)
- Customs forms completed
- Country-specific requirements
- Declared value accurate
- Harmonized codes
- No prohibited items

========================================
SHIPPING PROCEDURES
========================================

CARRIER PICKUP SCHEDULE:

DAILY PICKUPS:
- UPS: 4:00 PM
- FedEx: 4:30 PM
- USPS: 3:00 PM

LTL FREIGHT:
- Schedule in advance
- 4-hour pickup window
- Appointment required
- Liftgate if needed

CUT-OFF TIMES:
Orders must be packed by:
- Ground shipping: 2:00 PM
- Next-day air: 12:00 PM
- International: 11:00 AM
- Same day: 10:00 AM

MANIFEST PROCESS:

END-OF-DAY PROCEDURE:
1. Close shipments in carrier system
2. Generate manifest report
3. Print manifest for each carrier
4. Verify package count matches manifest
5. Sign and date manifest
6. Provide to carrier driver
7. File copy in shipping office
8. Email copy to accounting

FREIGHT SHIPMENTS:

LTL (Less Than Truckload):

PREPARING FREIGHT:
1. Palletize properly
2. Stretch wrap securely (min 3 layers)
3. Create BOL:
   - Origin and destination
   - Number of pallets
   - Total weight
   - Freight class
   - Special requirements
4. Attach freight labels to each pallet
5. Place BOL in protective pouch
6. Stage at dock door

FREIGHT CLASSES:
- Class 50: Dense, heavy items
- Class 100: Medium density
- Class 150: Light, bulky
- Class 250: Very light, large

Verify freight class for accurate billing

INSPECTION BEFORE LOADING:
□ Pallet in good condition
□ Load stable and secure
□ Proper wrapping
□ Labels visible
□ Correct weight documented
□ Freight class verified
□ Special handling noted

CARRIER DOCUMENTATION:
- Get driver signature on BOL
- Provide driver with all copies
- Retain warehouse copy
- Photograph loaded truck
- Note truck/trailer number
- Record seal number if used

SHIPPING CLAIMS:

DAMAGED IN TRANSIT:
Customer must:
1. Report within 48 hours
2. Provide photographs
3. Keep all packaging
4. File claim with carrier
5. We provide documentation

LOST SHIPMENTS:
1. Confirm tracking shows lost
2. File trace request
3. Wait 5 business days
4. File claim if not found
5. Reship if covered

WRONG ADDRESS:
1. Request address correction
2. Additional fees may apply
3. Update customer address
4. Notify customer service

========================================
RETURNS PROCESSING
========================================

RETURN AUTHORIZATION (RMA):

REQUIRED INFORMATION:
- RMA number from customer service
- Original order number
- Reason for return
- Return shipping tracking
- Expected arrival date

RECEIVING RETURNS:

STEP 1: CHECK IN
1. Verify RMA number
2. Inspect package condition
3. Photograph exterior
4. Note any damage

STEP 2: OPEN AND INSPECT
1. Verify contents match RMA
2. Check product condition:
   - New/unused
   - Like new
   - Used
   - Damaged/defective
3. Photograph items
4. Complete condition report

STEP 3: DISPOSITION
Based on condition:

NEW/UNUSED:
- Return to stock
- Full credit to customer

LIKE NEW:
- Minor inspection/cleaning
- Return to stock
- Full credit

USED:
- Evaluate resalability
- Possible restocking fee
- Partial credit

DAMAGED/DEFECTIVE:
- Quarantine
- Send to manufacturer
- Full credit if under warranty

STEP 4: PROCESS RETURN
1. Update WMS
2. Issue credit memo
3. Notify customer service
4. Add to inventory or quarantine
5. Close RMA

========================================
INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
========================================

STOCK ROTATION:

FIFO ENFORCEMENT:
- Oldest stock picked first
- Date all incoming stock
- Regular rotation checks
- Monthly expiration audits

EXPIRATION DATE POLICY:
- Quarantine 90 days before expiration
- Discount 60 days before
- Dispose 30 days before
- Never ship expired product

DEAD STOCK:
Review quarterly:
- No movement in 12 months
- Consider liquidation
- Clearance pricing
- Donation if appropriate

MINIMUM/MAXIMUM LEVELS:

REORDER POINTS:
Set based on:
- Average daily usage
- Lead time
- Safety stock
- Seasonal variations

REPLENISHMENT:
- Automatic for A items
- Manual review for B/C items
- Emergency orders as needed
- Communicate delays immediately

PHYSICAL INVENTORY:

ANNUAL FULL COUNT:
Scheduled annually:
1. Close warehouse operations
2. Assign count areas
3. Blind count (no reference)
4. Two-person verification
5. Enter counts in system
6. Investigate variances
7. Adjust inventory
8. Root cause analysis

SAFETY PROCEDURES
========================================

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES:

FIRE:
1. Activate alarm
2. Evacuate immediately
3. Call 911
4. Meet at assembly point
5. Account for all personnel

INJURY:
1. Secure area
2. Call for first aid (Extension 5222)
3. Call 911 if serious
4. Do not move injured person
5. Complete incident report

SPILL:
1. Contain if safe
2. Use spill kit
3. Notify supervisor
4. Follow MSDS procedures
5. Dispose properly

HAZMAT STORAGE:

SEGREGATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Acids separate from bases
- Oxidizers separate from flammables
- Compressed gases secured
- Proper ventilation
- Spill containment
- Fire suppression
- Emergency equipment accessible

FORKLIFT SAFETY:

OPERATOR REQUIREMENTS:
- Valid certification
- Daily inspection completed
- Seat belt worn
- Speed limits observed
- Horn used at intersections
- No riders allowed

ERGONOMICS:

LIFTING TECHNIQUES:
- Bend knees, not back
- Keep load close
- No twisting while lifting
- Team lift over 50 lbs
- Mechanical assistance when possible

REPETITIVE MOTION:
- Vary tasks
- Take breaks
- Stretch regularly
- Report discomfort early

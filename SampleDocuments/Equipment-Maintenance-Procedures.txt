EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES
Standard Operating Procedure
Document ID: SOP-MAINT-001
Version 2.0 - January 2025

========================================
PURPOSE
========================================

This document outlines the preventive maintenance procedures for all production and material handling equipment at our facility. Proper maintenance ensures:

- Equipment reliability and uptime
- Worker safety
- Cost efficiency
- Compliance with regulations
- Extended equipment life

========================================
SCOPE
========================================

This procedure applies to:
- All production equipment
- Material handling equipment (forklifts, pallet jacks)
- HVAC systems
- Electrical systems
- Building infrastructure
- Safety equipment

========================================
GENERAL MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES
========================================

LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO):
REQUIRED before maintenance on any energized equipment

PROCEDURE:
1. Notify affected employees
2. Shut down equipment properly
3. Isolate all energy sources:
   - Electrical
   - Hydraulic
   - Pneumatic
   - Mechanical
   - Thermal
4. Apply lockout devices
5. Attach personal danger tag
6. Verify zero energy state
7. Perform maintenance
8. Remove locks only after completion
9. Verify safe operation before restart

LOTO REQUIREMENTS:
- Personal locks for each worker
- "Danger - Do Not Operate" tags
- Group lockout for multiple workers
- Annual LOTO training required

WORK ORDER SYSTEM:
All maintenance must have work order

CREATING WORK ORDER:
1. Log into maintenance portal
2. Select equipment from dropdown
3. Describe issue in detail
4. Select priority level:
   - Emergency (safety hazard, complete shutdown)
   - Urgent (affecting production)
   - Routine (scheduled PM)
   - Low (cosmetic, convenience)
5. Attach photos if applicable
6. Submit for approval

WORK ORDER TRACKING:
- Open: Awaiting assignment
- In Progress: Technician working
- Parts Ordered: Waiting for materials
- On Hold: Pending approval/resources
- Completed: Work finished
- Closed: Verified and documented

========================================
DAILY OPERATOR INSPECTIONS
========================================

TO BE COMPLETED BEFORE EACH SHIFT

FORKLIFT INSPECTION:

VISUAL CHECK:
□ Check for leaks (oil, coolant, hydraulic)
□ Inspect tires for damage or wear
□ Check forks for cracks or bends
□ Verify all decals and labels present
□ Inspect chains and cables
□ Check fluid levels

OPERATIONAL CHECK:
□ Test horn
□ Check all lights
□ Test brakes
□ Verify steering response
□ Test hydraulic controls
□ Check backup alarm
□ Test emergency shut-off

IF ANY ISSUES FOUND:
1. Tag equipment "OUT OF SERVICE"
2. Complete deficiency report
3. Notify supervisor immediately
4. Use alternate equipment
5. Do not operate until repaired

PRODUCTION EQUIPMENT INSPECTION:

BEFORE STARTUP:
□ Clear work area
□ Check guards and safety devices
□ Verify emergency stops function
□ Inspect power cords and connections
□ Check for unusual noises or vibrations
□ Verify all tools and fixtures secure
□ Check coolant/lubricant levels

DURING OPERATION:
□ Monitor for unusual sounds
□ Watch for vibration changes
□ Check temperature indicators
□ Observe product quality
□ Monitor cycle times
□ Note any error messages

AT SHIFT END:
□ Clean equipment thoroughly
□ Wipe down surfaces
□ Remove debris and waste
□ Check for loose components
□ Report any concerns
□ Complete operator log

========================================
SPECIFIC EQUIPMENT PROCEDURES
========================================

ELECTRIC FORKLIFT BATTERY MAINTENANCE

DAILY:
- Check battery charge level
- Inspect for damage or corrosion
- Verify proper installation

WEEKLY:
- Check electrolyte levels
- Add distilled water if needed (only when fully charged)
- Clean terminals
- Check voltage

MONTHLY:
- Perform equalization charge
- Check specific gravity of cells
- Clean battery compartment
- Inspect cables and connections
- Document readings

BATTERY ROOM PROCEDURES:
- Eyewash station accessible
- PPE required (face shield, gloves, apron)
- Acid spill kit available
- No smoking or open flames
- Adequate ventilation

CHARGING PROCEDURES:
1. Park in designated charging area
2. Turn off forklift
3. Connect charger (red to positive, black to negative)
4. Start charging cycle
5. Monitor for overheating
6. Disconnect when complete
7. Record charge time and date

AIR COMPRESSOR MAINTENANCE

DAILY:
- Check pressure gauge
- Drain moisture from tank
- Check for leaks
- Verify auto-shutoff functions

WEEKLY:
- Clean air intake filter
- Check oil level
- Inspect drive belt
- Test safety relief valve

MONTHLY:
- Replace air filter
- Change oil (per hours of operation)
- Check all connections
- Inspect pressure switch
- Clean cooling fins

QUARTERLY:
- Comprehensive leak check
- Replace drive belt if worn
- Check motor bearings
- Test all safety devices
- Document condition

HVAC SYSTEM MAINTENANCE

MONTHLY:
- Replace air filters
- Check thermostat operation
- Inspect condensate drain
- Check refrigerant levels
- Clean condenser coils

QUARTERLY:
- Professional service
- Check electrical connections
- Lubricate motors
- Inspect ductwork
- Test all controls

ANNUALLY:
- Complete system inspection
- Efficiency testing

- Electrical system check

========================================
LUBRICATION PROCEDURES
========================================

GENERAL LUBRICATION GUIDELINES:

GREASE POINTS:
- Identify all grease fittings
- Clean fitting before greasing
- Use appropriate grease type
- Pump until clean grease appears
- Wipe excess grease
- Document lubrication date

LUBRICATION SCHEDULE:
- Daily: High-use pivot points
- Weekly: General grease points
- Monthly: All moving components
- Quarterly: Comprehensive lubrication

LUBRICANT TYPES:
- EP2 Grease: General purpose
- High-temp Grease: Over 200°F
- Food-grade: Food contact areas
- Anti-seize: Threaded connections
- Way Oil: Machine slides
- Hydraulic Oil: ISO 32 or 46

OIL CHANGES:

EQUIPMENT TYPE | INTERVAL | OIL TYPE
---------------|----------|----------
Forklifts      | 250 hrs  | 10W-30 or 15W-40
Air Compressor | 500 hrs  | ISO 46 Synthetic
Hydraulic      | 1000 hrs | ISO 32 Hydraulic
Gearboxes      | 2000 hrs | ISO 220 Gear Oil

OIL CHANGE PROCEDURE:
1. Run equipment to operating temp
2. Shut down and lockout
3. Position drain pan
4. Remove drain plug
5. Allow complete drainage (30+ min)
6. Replace drain plug and gasket
7. Fill with new oil to proper level
8. Run equipment and check level
9. Check for leaks
10. Document oil change

========================================
MAINTENANCE METRICS
========================================

KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS:

EQUIPMENT UPTIME:
Target: 95% or higher
Calculation: (Operating Hours / Total Hours) × 100

MEAN TIME BETWEEN FAILURES (MTBF):
Target: 500+ hours
Calculation: Total Operating Time / Number of Failures

MEAN TIME TO REPAIR (MTTR):
Target: Less than 4 hours
Calculation: Total Repair Time / Number of Repairs

PREVENTIVE VS. CORRECTIVE RATIO:
Target: 80% preventive, 20% corrective
Calculation: (PM Hours / Total Maint Hours) × 100

MAINTENANCE COST PER UNIT:
Target: Specific to equipment
Calculation: Total Maint Cost / Units Produced

REPORTING:
- Weekly: Downtime report
- Monthly: KPI dashboard
- Quarterly: Equipment condition reports
- Annually: Maintenance budget review

========================================
SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
========================================

PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT:

MINIMUM REQUIRED:
- Safety glasses
- Steel-toed boots
- Hearing protection (if over 85 dB)
- Work gloves

TASK-SPECIFIC:
- Welding: Welding helmet, leather gloves
- Chemical: Chemical-resistant gloves, face shield
- Electrical: Insulated gloves, arc-rated clothing
- Confined Space: Respirator, harness

CONFINED SPACE ENTRY:
Required for tanks, pits, vessels

PROCEDURE:
1. Obtain entry permit
2. Test atmosphere (O2, LEL, toxic gases)
3. Establish ventilation
4. Post attendant at entrance
5. Use retrieval equipment
6. Maintain communication
7. Emergency rescue plan ready

ELECTRICAL SAFETY:
- Qualified electrician only
- Lockout/tagout required
- Insulated tools
- Arc-rated PPE
- No energized work unless justified

========================================
EMERGENCY MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES
========================================

EQUIPMENT FAILURE DURING OPERATION:

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Press emergency stop
2. Secure work area
3. Evacuate if dangerous
4. Call maintenance (Extension 5100)
5. Notify supervisor
6. Complete incident report

PRIORITY RESPONSE:
- Safety hazards: Within 15 minutes
- Production stoppage: Within 30 minutes
- Partial operation: Within 2 hours
- Non-critical: Next business day

TEMPORARY REPAIRS:
- Authorized only if safe
- Document temporary fix
- Schedule permanent repair
- Monitor closely
- Post warning signs

EQUIPMENT REPLACEMENT CRITERIA:
Consider replacement when:
- Repair cost > 50% of replacement cost
- Frequent breakdowns (3+ per month)
- Parts no longer available
- Safety cannot be assured
- Technology obsolete
- Maintenance cost exceeds depreciation

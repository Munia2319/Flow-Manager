1. EMPLOYMENT BASICS
========================================

EQUAL OPPORTUNITY EMPLOYER
We are committed to providing equal employment opportunities regardless of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or veteran status.

EMPLOYMENT CLASSIFICATION

Full-Time Employees:
- Work 40 hours per week
- Eligible for all benefits
- Receive paid time off
- Health insurance coverage

Part-Time Employees:
- Work less than 30 hours per week
- Limited benefits eligibility
- Prorated time off benefits
- No health insurance

Temporary/Seasonal:
- Fixed-term employment
- Not eligible for benefits
- May be converted to regular status

PROBATIONARY PERIOD
- All new employees: 90-day probationary period
- Performance reviewed at 30, 60, and 90 days
- Full benefits begin after probation
- Employment may be terminated during probation

2. TIME OFF AND LEAVE POLICIES
========================================

PAID TIME OFF (PTO)

Full-Time Employees:
- Years 0-2: 15 days per year
- Years 3-5: 20 days per year
- Years 6-10: 25 days per year
- Years 10+: 30 days per year

Part-Time Employees:
- 10 days per year (prorated)

PTO ACCRUAL:
- Accrues each pay period
- Maximum rollover: 40 hours per year
- Must be used within 12 months
- Cannot be cashed out except at termination

PTO REQUEST PROCESS:
1. Submit request in employee portal
2. Minimum 2 weeks notice for 3+ days off
3. Supervisor approval required
4. Holiday blackout periods apply
5. First-come, first-served for peak periods

SICK LEAVE

Full-Time Employees:
- 10 sick days per year
- Does not roll over
- Medical documentation required for 3+ consecutive days
- May use for family member illness

Part-Time Employees:
- 5 sick days per year (prorated)

HOLIDAYS

Paid Holidays (Full-Time Only):
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Eve (half day)
- Christmas Day
- New Year's Eve (half day)

Total: 10 paid holidays

Holiday pay: Regular hourly rate × 8 hours

PARENTAL LEAVE
- 12 weeks paid leave (primary caregiver)
- 6 weeks paid leave (secondary caregiver)
- Additional 12 weeks unpaid FMLA available
- Must be employed 12+ months
- Return-to-work guarantee

BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
- 5 days paid leave (immediate family)
- 3 days paid leave (extended family)
- Additional unpaid time may be approved

JURY DUTY
- Paid leave for jury service
- Submit jury summons to HR
- Return to work if dismissed early

MILITARY LEAVE
- Unpaid leave for military service
- Job protection per USERRA
- Benefits continuation available

3. WORK SCHEDULE AND ATTENDANCE
========================================

STANDARD HOURS
Full-Time: 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Monday - Friday)
- 1-hour unpaid lunch break
- Two 15-minute paid breaks

Shift Work:
- First Shift: 6:00 AM - 2:30 PM
- Second Shift: 2:30 PM - 11:00 PM
- Third Shift: 11:00 PM - 7:30 AM
- Shift differential: $2/hour (2nd shift), $3/hour (3rd shift)

ATTENDANCE POLICY
- Punctuality is essential
- Clock in/out required for hourly employees
- Notify supervisor if late (before shift start)
- More than 3 unexcused absences = written warning

TARDINESS:
- Late: Arrival after scheduled start time
- 3 tardies = 1 unexcused absence
- Excessive tardiness = disciplinary action

ATTENDANCE POINTS SYSTEM:
- Unexcused absence: 1 point
- Tardy (under 2 hours): 0.5 points
- No-call/no-show: 3 points
- 8 points in 12 months: Termination

OVERTIME
- Requires supervisor pre-approval
- Paid at 1.5× regular rate (over 40 hours/week)
- Mandatory overtime may be required
- Refusal of mandatory overtime = disciplinary action

REMOTE WORK POLICY
Eligible Positions Only:
- Requires manager approval
- Hybrid schedule: 3 days office, 2 days remote
- Must maintain productivity standards
- Equipment provided by company
- Home office must meet safety requirements

4. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
========================================

PAYROLL
- Pay periods: Bi-weekly (every other Friday)
- Direct deposit required
- Pay stubs available in employee portal
- Year-end W-2 forms available by January 31

HEALTH INSURANCE
Full-Time Employees (after probation):

Medical Insurance:
- Company pays 80% of premium
- Employee pays 20%
- Coverage effective 1st of month after hire
- HSA-eligible plan available
- Annual open enrollment in November

Dental Insurance:
- Company pays 50% of premium
- Coverage for employee and dependents
- Preventive care covered 100%

Vision Insurance:
- $10/month employee contribution
- Annual eye exam covered
- Discounts on glasses/contacts

RETIREMENT PLAN
401(k) Retirement Savings:
- Eligible after 6 months
- Company match: 50% up to 6% contribution
- Vesting: Immediate for employee contributions
- Company match vests after 3 years
- Financial planning services available

LIFE INSURANCE
- Company-paid: 1× annual salary
- Additional coverage available at employee cost
- Accidental Death & Dismemberment included

DISABILITY INSURANCE
- Short-term: 60% salary up to 12 weeks
- Long-term: 60% salary after 90 days
- Waiting period: 14 days

EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (EAP)
- Free confidential counseling (8 sessions/year)
- Legal consultation
- Financial planning assistance
- Family support services
- 24/7 hotline: 1-800-EAP-HELP

ADDITIONAL BENEFITS
- Gym membership reimbursement: $50/month
- Professional development: $1,000/year
- Tuition reimbursement: Up to $5,250/year
- Employee referral bonus: $1,000
- Service awards (5, 10, 15, 20, 25 years)

5. WORKPLACE CONDUCT
========================================

CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees must:
- Treat others with respect
- Follow all safety procedures
- Protect company property
- Maintain confidentiality
- Report ethical concerns
- Avoid conflicts of interest

DRESS CODE
Business Casual:
- Collared shirts or blouses
- Slacks, khakis, or professional skirts
- Closed-toe shoes
- Clean, professional appearance

Safety Areas (Warehouse/Production):
- Steel-toed boots required
- Long pants (no shorts)
- Safety glasses in designated areas
- High-visibility vest required
- Hair tied back, no jewelry

Casual Friday:
- Jeans allowed
- Company logo shirts acceptable
- No torn or excessively worn clothing

PROHIBITED:
- Tank tops, shorts (office areas)
- Flip-flops, sandals (safety areas)
- Offensive graphics or language
- Excessively casual attire

ANTI-HARASSMENT POLICY
Zero tolerance for:
- Sexual harassment
- Discrimination
- Bullying or intimidation
- Retaliation

REPORTING PROCEDURE:
1. Report to supervisor or HR immediately
2. Investigation conducted within 5 business days
3. Confidentiality maintained
4. No retaliation against complainants
5. Disciplinary action up to termination

SUBSTANCE ABUSE POLICY
Prohibited:
- Alcohol consumption during work hours
- Illegal drug use
- Working under the influence
- Possession of controlled substances

Drug Testing:
- Pre-employment screening
- Random testing (safety-sensitive positions)
- Post-accident testing
- Reasonable suspicion testing

Violation = Immediate termination

CONFIDENTIALITY
Employees must protect:
- Customer information
- Financial data
- Trade secrets
- Employee personal information
- Proprietary processes

Breach of confidentiality = Immediate termination

========================================
6. SAFETY AND SECURITY
========================================

WORKPLACE SAFETY
- Report all hazards immediately
- Use required PPE in designated areas
- Follow all safety procedures
- Report injuries within 24 hours
- Complete annual safety training

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
Fire:
1. Activate fire alarm
2. Evacuate building
3. Meet at designated assembly point
4. Do not re-enter until authorized

Medical Emergency:
1. Call 911
2. Notify first aid team
3. Render aid if trained
4. Complete incident report

WORKERS' COMPENSATION
- Report injuries immediately to supervisor
- Medical treatment at designated facilities
- Complete incident report within 24 hours
- Light duty available during recovery
- Protected from retaliation

SECURITY
- Employee ID badge required at all times
- Sign in visitors
- Lock workstations when away
- Report suspicious activity
- No unauthorized facility access

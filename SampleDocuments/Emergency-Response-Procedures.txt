GENERAL EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
========================================

IN ANY EMERGENCY:
1. REMAIN CALM
2. ASSESS THE SITUATION
3. PROTECT YOURSELF FIRST
4. CALL FOR HELP (911 and/or Extension 5911)
5. FOLLOW EMERGENCY TEAM INSTRUCTIONS
6. EVACUATE IF DIRECTED
7. ACCOUNT FOR ALL PERSONNEL

DO NOT:
- Use elevators during emergencies
- Re-enter building without authorization
- Block emergency exits or routes
- Ignore alarms or warnings
- Take unnecessary risks

========================================
FIRE EMERGENCY
========================================

IF YOU DISCOVER A FIRE:

SMALL FIRE (Size of waste basket or smaller):
1. Activate nearest fire alarm pull station
2. Use fire extinguisher if trained (P.A.S.S. method)
   - Pull pin
   - Aim at base of fire
   - Squeeze handle
   - Sweep side to side
3. If fire spreads, evacuate immediately
4. Close doors behind you

LARGE FIRE:
1. Activate fire alarm immediately
2. Call 911
3. Evacuate building using nearest safe exit
4. DO NOT attempt to fight large fires
5. Alert others as you evacuate
6. Close doors to contain fire

WHEN FIRE ALARM SOUNDS:
1. Stop work immediately
2. Turn off equipment if safe to do so
3. Grab personal belongings if immediately accessible
4. Use nearest marked exit
5. Walk quickly, do not run
6. Feel doors before opening (if hot, use alternate route)
7. Stay low if smoke present
8. Proceed to assembly point
9. Report to team leader for headcount

IF TRAPPED IN BUILDING:
1. Go to room with window
2. Close door, seal cracks with cloth
3. Call 911 and give exact location
4. Signal from window
5. Stay low to avoid smoke
6. Wait for rescue

FIRE EXTINGUISHER LOCATIONS:
- All exits
- Break rooms
- Equipment rooms
- Production areas (every 50 feet)
- Offices (every 100 feet)

========================================
MEDICAL EMERGENCY
========================================

FOR SERIOUS INJURIES/ILLNESS:

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Call 911 immediately
2. Call Extension 5222 (First Aid Team)
3. Do not move injured person unless in immediate danger
4. Stay with person until help arrives
5. Provide basic first aid if trained
6. Keep person calm and comfortable

MINOR INJURIES:
1. Report to First Aid Station (Building A, Room 101)
2. Notify supervisor
3. Complete incident report
4. Follow up medical care if needed

FIRST AID TEAM MEMBERS:
- Maria Chen (Extension 5223) - Building A
- James Rodriguez (Extension 5224) - Building B
- Sarah Johnson (Extension 5225) - Warehouse

AED (AUTOMATED EXTERNAL DEFIBRILLATOR) LOCATIONS:
- Main lobby near reception
- Break room (Building A)
- Warehouse office
- Training room (Building B)

ALLERGIC REACTIONS:
1. Call 911 if severe (difficulty breathing, swelling)
2. Ask if person has EpiPen
3. Assist with medication if needed
4. Keep person calm and lying down
5. Monitor breathing

CHOKING:
1. Ask "Are you choking?"
2. If cannot speak/breathe, perform Heimlich maneuver
3. Call 911 if unconscious
4. Begin CPR if trained and necessary

========================================
SEVERE WEATHER
========================================

TORNADO WARNING:

When Tornado Siren Sounds:
1. Move immediately to designated shelter areas
2. Stay away from windows
3. Go to lowest floor, interior rooms
4. Get under sturdy furniture if possible
5. Cover head and neck
6. Remain in shelter until "all clear" given

TORNADO SHELTER LOCATIONS:
- Building A: Basement storage area
- Building B: Interior hallway, first floor
- Warehouse: Interior offices, north side

FLOODING:
1. Move to higher ground immediately
2. Avoid walking through water (6 inches can knock you down)
3. Do not drive through flooded areas
4. Turn off electrical equipment
5. Follow evacuation orders

SEVERE THUNDERSTORM:
1. Suspend outdoor activities
2. Stay away from windows
3. Unplug sensitive equipment
4. Avoid using landline phones
5. Wait 30 minutes after last thunder to resume outdoor work

========================================
CHEMICAL SPILL
========================================

MINOR SPILL (Less than 1 gallon, non-hazardous):
1. Alert nearby personnel
2. Retrieve spill kit from storage
3. Wear appropriate PPE (gloves, goggles)
4. Contain spill with absorbent material
5. Clean up following MSDS guidelines
6. Dispose in proper waste container
7. Report to supervisor

MAJOR SPILL OR HAZARDOUS MATERIAL:
1. Evacuate immediate area
2. Call Extension 5911 and 911
3. Activate fire alarm if flammable
4. Close doors to contain spill
5. Notify supervisor and safety officer
6. Provide MSDS information to responders
7. Evacuate building if directed

SPILL KIT LOCATIONS:
- Chemical storage areas
- Production floor (4 locations)
- Maintenance shop
- Loading dock

WHAT TO REPORT:
- Material spilled (name and MSDS number)
- Approximate quantity
- Location
- Any injuries
- Fire or explosion risk

CHEMICAL EXPOSURE:
Eyes:
- Flush with water for 15 minutes
- Call 911
- Seek immediate medical attention

Skin:
- Remove contaminated clothing
- Flush with water for 15 minutes
- Wash with mild soap
- Seek medical attention

Inhalation:
- Move to fresh air immediately
- Call 911 if breathing difficulty
- Loosen tight clothing
- Keep person calm

========================================
ACTIVE THREAT/VIOLENCE
========================================

RUN - HIDE - FIGHT (in that order)

RUN:
- If safe exit route exists
- Leave belongings behind
- Help others if possible
- Call 911 when safe

HIDE:
- If evacuation not possible
- Lock and barricade doors
- Turn off lights
- Silence phones
- Hide behind large objects
- Remain quiet
- Call 911 if safe to do so

FIGHT:
- As LAST RESORT only
- Commit to actions
- Act with aggression
- Use improvised weapons
- Work together

AFTER INCIDENT:
- Follow law enforcement instructions
- Keep hands visible
- Avoid sudden movements
- Provide information calmly
- Seek medical/psychological help

THREAT REPORTING:
Report concerning behaviors to:
- Security: Extension 5911
- HR: Extension 5000
- Anonymous Hotline: 1-800-555-0199

========================================
POWER OUTAGE
========================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:
1. Remain calm and stay in place
2. Emergency lighting will activate
3. Unplug sensitive equipment
4. Wait for instructions
5. Facilities will assess situation

IF EXTENDED OUTAGE:
1. Save work if possible
2. Shut down equipment properly
3. Follow supervisor instructions
4. May be sent home with pay
5. Check company website/hotline for updates

ELEVATOR ENTRAPMENT:
1. Stay calm
2. Use emergency phone in elevator
3. Do not attempt to exit
4. Wait for rescue
5. Help is on the way

========================================
EARTHQUAKE
========================================

DURING EARTHQUAKE:

INDOORS:
1. DROP to ground
2. Take COVER under desk/table
3. HOLD ON until shaking stops
4. Stay away from windows
5. Do not use elevators
6. Protect head and neck

OUTDOORS:
1. Move away from buildings
2. Avoid power lines
3. Drop to ground
4. Cover head
5. Wait until shaking stops

AFTER EARTHQUAKE:
1. Check for injuries
2. Inspect for hazards
3. Expect aftershocks
4. Evacuate if building damaged
5. Do not use elevators
6. Check in at assembly point

========================================
GAS LEAK
========================================

IF YOU SMELL GAS:

1. DO NOT turn lights on/off
2. DO NOT use phones in area
3. DO NOT create sparks
4. Evacuate building immediately
5. Call 911 from safe location
6. Alert others
7. Do not re-enter until cleared

NATURAL GAS PROPERTIES:
- Rotten egg smell (added odorant)
- Highly flammable
- Explosive in enclosed spaces

========================================
BOMB THREAT
========================================

IF YOU RECEIVE BOMB THREAT CALL:

STAY CALM AND:
1. Keep caller on line as long as possible
2. Note exact wording of threat
3. Ask questions:
   - Where is the bomb?
   - When will it explode?
   - What does it look like?
   - Why did you place it?
   - Who are you?
4. Note caller details:
   - Gender
   - Accent
   - Background noise
   - Emotional state
5. Call 911 immediately after
6. Notify security (Extension 5911)
7. Do not hang up - alert someone else

EVACUATION DECISION:
Made by:
- Law enforcement
- Facility manager
- Emergency coordinator

DO NOT:
- Touch suspicious packages
- Use radios or cell phones near suspected bomb
- Re-enter until cleared

========================================
POST-EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
========================================

AFTER ANY EMERGENCY:

1. HEADCOUNT: Report to team leader at assembly point
2. INJURIES: Report all injuries to supervisor
3. INCIDENT REPORT: Complete within 24 hours
4. COUNSELING: EAP services available (1-800-EAP-HELP)
5. DEBRIEFING: Attend mandatory safety meeting
6. DOCUMENTATION: Preserve evidence if needed

RETURN TO WORK:
- Wait for "all clear" from authorities
- Inspect workspace for hazards
- Report damage to facilities
- Resume normal operations when safe
